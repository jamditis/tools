Parchment is a study in material honesty. We prioritize the physical qualities of communication—contrast, weight, and the tactile nature of the written word.

01 / Motion kinetics

Style guidelines for movement

Staggered entrance

Elements should never appear simultaneously. Use a 100ms offset to simulate organic discovery.

Ink spreads

Interactive states should feel like ink soaking into paper. Use soft blurs and scale-ups.

Ambient drift

Floating light

Large empty spaces should contain slow-moving atmospheric light to prevent a static feel.

02 / Responsive standard

Adaptive layout rules

The fluid typography floor

On mobile, our large display type scales to 14vw. This ensures that titles remain a design element rather than just text, but never clip the viewport boundaries.
